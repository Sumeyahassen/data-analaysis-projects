Project Goals & Objectives
The primary purpose of this project is divided into two key pillars: Technical Prediction and Strategic Business Value.

1. Technical & Analytical Goal
Data Integration & Modeling: Merge disparate customer demographic data (client.csv) and behavioral/usage records (record.csv) using Customer_ID to create a unified, comprehensive customer profile.

Churn Prediction: Train and evaluate advanced Machine Learning classification models (such as Random Forest or XGBoost) to accurately predict whether a customer is at high risk of churning within 31–60 days.

2. Ultimate Business Goal (Value Creation)
Proactive Risk Identification: Enable Company A—a telecommunications operator lacking in-house machine learning capabilities—to automatically identify which specific customers are likely to cancel their services and why.

Actionable Business Proposal: Translate technical model predictions into a concrete, data-driven business proposal. This empowers management to deploy targeted retention strategies (e.g., customized discounts, device upgrades, or service adjustments) for high-risk segments, thereby minimizing revenue loss, reducing churn rates, and maximizing overall customer lifetime value.
