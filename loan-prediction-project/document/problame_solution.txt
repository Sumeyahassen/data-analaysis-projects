Project Overview: Loan Default Prediction System1. 
1.The Core Technical ProblemWhat we are trying to solve: Can we accurately 
    predict whether a loan applicant will successfully repay their loan (0) or 
    default on their payment (1) based on their historical and financial profile?
    Type of Problem: Binary Classification (supervised machine learning where the 
    target variable is categorical with two outcomes: Default = 0 or 1).
2.The Business Problem & MotivationFinancial Risk & Losses: When banks or lending
    institutions issue loans, their primary risk is credit default—borrowers failing 
    to repay, which leads to massive financial losses and non-performing assets (NPAs).
    Manual Inefficiency & Bias: Traditional manual credit review processes are slow,
    subjective, and prone to human error or bias, making it difficult to scale 
    lending operations safely.The Goal: Build an automated, data-driven credit risk 
    assessment model that helps the bank approve reliable borrowers instantly while 
    flagging high-risk applicants before loans are disbursed.
3.The Proposed SolutionData-Driven Intelligence: Build an end-to-end machine learning 
    pipeline that analyzes applicant features—such as annual income, credit score, debt-to-income 
    ratio (DTIRatio), employment history (MonthsEmployed), and loan terms.
    Predictive Modeling: Train classification algorithms (like Random Forest, XGBoost, or Logistic
    Regression) to learn patterns from historical loan data.Operational Impact: Provide the bank 
    with a robust decision-support tool that minimizes default rates, optimizes interest rates,
    and speeds up loan approval workflows.
